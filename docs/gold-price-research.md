## 5. Formula

```
Price = (spot per gram) × (karat purity) × (weight in grams) + premium
```

Examples (EUR, no premium):
- 24K: 113.01 × 0.999 × 1000 = €112,899
- 22K: 113.01 × 0.916 × 1000 = €103,519
- 18K: 113.01 × 0.750 × 1000 = €84,759
- 14K: 113.01 × 0.585 × 1000 = €66,112
