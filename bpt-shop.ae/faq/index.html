For transactions that reach or exceed the threshold of 55,000 AED, we are legally required to verify the source of funds. This can be done through pay stubs, bank statements, or sales documents.

The term “Investment Grade” refers to precious metals that are officially recognized as investment assets. In the United Arab Emirates (UAE), this is the legal requirement for VAT-free (0% VAT) purchases.

According to the legal provisions in Dubai, for gold or silver to be considered an “Investment Precious Metal,” the following minimum requirements must be met by law:

  1. Purity: Gold must have a fineness of at least 99% (990/1000) and silver of at least 99.9% (999/1000).
  2. Form: The metal must be in the form of bars or coins that are tradable on international markets. Jewelry or industrial metals do not qualify for this tax exemption.
  3. Trading standard: Products must come from certified manufacturers (e.g., LBMA standard).

We place the highest value on quality and value retention. To guarantee our customers “German precision,” we adhere to the highest standards:

  • Primary product range (fine gold & fine silver): We primarily carry products with the highest fineness of 999.9/1000 (24 karat for gold). These products are considered “investment grade” and are completely VAT-exempt within Dubai (0% VAT).
  • Disclosure of deviations: If we carry products whose fineness falls below the legal thresholds for tax exemption, we clearly indicate this in the shop.
  • Tax Note: Please note that products that do not meet the legal minimum requirements for investment-grade precious metals (99% for gold / 99.9% for silver) are subject to the statutory 5% VAT. This will be clearly displayed in your shopping cart.

Thanks to this clear labeling, when you shop at Bullion Portfolio Trading LLC, you always do so with full transparency regarding quality and tax status.
