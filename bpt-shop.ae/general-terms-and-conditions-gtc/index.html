GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS (GTC)

for Trading in Precious Metals (Gold & Silber)

Scope of Application: Jurisdiction: Dubai, United Arab Emirates

As of June 22, 2026

1. General and Scope of Application

These Terms and Conditions (hereinafter “GTC”) apply to all contracts concluded between Bullion Portfolio Trading LLC, located at Marasi Drive, 12th Floor, Office 1204 | Business Bay | Dubai, UAE (hereinafter “Seller” or “we”) and the customer (hereinafter “Customer” or “Buyer”).

Geographic Restriction: Our offer as well as the execution of contracts are subject to strict geographic restrictions: Exclusive Delivery Area: At the present time, we offer our goods exclusively to customers within the city area of Dubai (UAE). Delivery to other Emirates or abroad is currently excluded.

In the event of violations of the geographical restrictions, we are entitled to reject the order or to terminate an already concluded contract for good cause. Already executed payments will be refunded – subject to any verification obligations under anti-money laundering regulations – less any verifiable costs incurred by us.

By submitting an order, the Customer acknowledges these GTC in the version valid at the time of the order as solely authoritative. Deviating conditions of the Customer shall not be recognized.

2. Subject Matter of the Contract and Price Fluctuations

The subject matter of the contract is the sale of precious metals (in particular gold coins, silver coins, bullion/bars). 

Important Notice on Prices: The Customer acknowledges that the prices for the offered goods (precious metals) are subject to fluctuations on the financial market over which the Seller has no influence. The prices displayed in the shop are updated at regular intervals (live pricing or spot price fixing). 

The final price of the goods is bindingly fixed at the time of the order (“Fixing”).

The decisive fixing time for the final price is the moment in which the order is completed and transmitted via the online system. The price determined at this point in time is binding for the Customer’s offer. This point in time may deviate slightly from the time of the Customer’s click. The price determined at the time of fixing remains valid until our acceptance or rejection of the order.

If we reject the order, no contract is concluded. Already executed payments will be refunded – subject to any verification obligations under anti-money laundering regulations.

A subsequent withdrawal from the purchase contract by the Customer is excluded due to market fluctuations (see Section 4: Exclusion of Right of Withdrawal / Cancellation).

3. Conclusion of Contract

The presentation of products in the online shop does not constitute a legally binding offer, but rather a non-binding online catalog. 

By clicking the button “Place Binding Order” (or a similar designation), the Customer places a binding order for the goods contained in the shopping cart. 

Due to the volatile nature of the precious metal market, the purchase contract is only concluded when we explicitly accept the order via an order confirmation email or when we dispatch the goods. An automatic confirmation of receipt does not constitute acceptance of the contract.

4. Exclusion of Right of Withdrawal / Cancellation

No Right of Withdrawal: Since the price of the contract goods (precious metals) depends on fluctuations in the financial market over which the Seller has no influence, there is no right of withdrawal or return for the Customer in accordance with established trade customs and legal provisions. Every purchase is final (“All sales are final”). 

Cancellations by the Customer after the conclusion of the contract are excluded. This does not affect the right of the Seller to agree to a cancellation out of goodwill in justified exceptional cases. The Customer has no legal claim to such a cancellation.

We reserve the right to withdraw from the contract if the goods cannot be delivered due to force majeure, supply shortages from the mints, or obvious pricing errors (technical errors in the data feed). In this case, payments already made will be refunded.

5. Prices, Terms of Payment, and Retention of Title

All prices are stated in UAE Dirhams (AED) unless explicitly marked otherwise.

Value Added Tax (VAT):

  • Investment Precious Metals: In accordance with UAE legislation (Executive Regulations), the delivery of investment gold, silver, and platinum with a purity of at least 99% (for gold) or 99.9% (for silver) is subject to a zero tax rate (0% VAT), provided they are internationally recognized bars or coins.
  • Other Products: For goods that do not meet the criteria for investment gold (e.g., jewelry or gold with a lower purity), the statutory UAE VAT of currently 5% will be levied and shown separately in the invoice amount.

Insofar as prices or transaction values are displayed in a foreign currency, the reference rate published by the Central Bank of the UAE on the day of the order shall be decisive for the conversion.

Payment is due immediately upon conclusion of the contract (advance payment). Accepted payment methods are displayed during checkout. If the due payment is not received by us within 48 hours after the conclusion of the contract, we are entitled to withdraw from the contract and offer the goods for sale again. 

The goods remain the property of the Seller until full payment of all claims arising from the purchase contract has been received.

6. Prevention of Money Laundering (KYC / AML Compliance)

Legal Basis: The Seller is registered as a Dealer in Precious Metals and Stones (DPMS) in the UAE and is strictly subject to the provisions of UAE Federal Decree-Law No. (20) of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism, as well as the associated Cabinet Decisions and guidelines issued by the Ministry of Economy. 

Identification Obligation (Know Your Customer – KYC): To fulfill our legal obligations, we are required to unambiguously verify the identity of our customers before concluding certain transactions.

  • Natural Persons: We require a clearly legible copy of a valid Emirates ID. Additionally, a current proof of address (e.g., utility bill, not older than 3 months) may be requested.
  • Legal Entities (Companies): We require the Trade License, the Certificate of Incorporation, Emirates ID copies of all directors, as well as identification verification of all Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs) who hold more than 25% of the shares.

Thresholds and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD): Regardless of the payment method, we reserve the right to carry out a full identity check for transactions exceeding a value of AED 55,000 (or the corresponding equivalent in foreign currency) – whether in a single transaction or in several linked transactions. For transactions with an increased risk profile (e.g., Politically Exposed Persons – PEPs or high-risk countries), enhanced due diligence (EDD) applies, which may require additional documentation.

Regardless of this threshold, we reserve the right to apply a full identity check and enhanced due diligence even for lower transaction amounts based on a risk-based approach.

Source of Funds / Wealth: The Seller reserves the right to request proof of the source of funds used for the purchase (“Source of Funds”) and, if applicable, the source of wealth (“Source of Wealth”). This can be done by submitting bank statements, salary slips, deeds of sale, or other official documents. If this proof is not provided or is insufficient, the transaction will be rejected.

Prohibition of Third-Party Payments: We accept payments exclusively from accounts held in the name of the ordering Customer. Payments from third parties who are not party to the contract will be rejected and transferred back to the sender – subject to any verification obligations under anti-money laundering regulations – less any bank fees. The name of the account holder must match the name of the invoice recipient. Refunds of such rejected third-party payments may be delayed insofar as and as long as this is necessary to fulfill statutory obligations in the field of prevention of money laundering and terrorist financing..

Sanctions List Screening: Every customer and every transaction is screened against national (UAE Local Terrorist List) and international sanctions lists (e.g., UN Security Council Consolidated List). We do not engage in business with individuals, companies, or countries listed on these sanctions lists.

Reporting Obligation and Transaction Termination: In the event of suspicion of money laundering, terrorist financing, or illegal activities, we are legally required to freeze or cancel the transaction without prior notice and to submit a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) or a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) to the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU). According to the statutory “anti-tipping-off” regulations, we are strictly prohibited from informing the Customer that a report has been filed.

Data Storage: All data and documents collected as part of the KYC and AML checks will be securely archived in accordance with the legal requirements of the UAE for a period of at least five (5) years after the termination of the business relationship or completion of the transaction.

Identification for Representation (Power of Attorney): If the Customer exercises their right to authorize a third party via a Power of Attorney (PoA) to receive the goods, this authorized person is also fully subject to the identification requirements (KYC). The Seller is legally required to request and verify a high-resolution copy of the authorized person’s Emirates ID prior to delivery. The granting of a Power of Attorney does not release the Buyer from their own KYC obligations under points 6.2 and 6.5 (Prohibition of Third-Party Payments).

Reservation of Rights in the Event of Negative or Refused KYC Verification: We reserve the express right to immediately cancel orders or to withdraw from an already concluded contract if a KYC or AML check is negative, the Customer refuses to cooperate, or fails to submit the requested proof and documentation in a timely or complete manner. In such cases, we further reserve the right to initiate legal action or to file reports with the competent regulatory authorities. In this event, any refund of payments already made will only be processed after final clearance by the competent compliance authorities and less all costs incurred by us.

7. Shipping and Transfer of Risk

Shipping Service Providers and Security Standards: The shipping of precious metals is carried out exclusively through specialized secure logistics partners (in particular Loomis International) or other highly specialized secure courier services. In accordance with the strict security guidelines and contractual agreements with our logistics partners, delivery to packing stations, post office boxes (P.O. Boxes), or unmonitored drop-off locations is categorically excluded. Delivery is only possible to the address stored in the order against a physical confirmation of receipt. The handover of the goods takes place exclusively against a physical receipt confirmation. In accordance with the “Know Your Customer” guidelines, the logistics partner is obliged to establish the identity of the recipient or the ultimate beneficial owner. The identity of the recipient must generally match the details of the Buyer in the purchase contract, unless a Power of Attorney for a third party has been confirmed in writing by the Seller in advance. Shipments are secured through our partner within the framework of the agreed maximum liability amounts (up to USD 15 million per shipment).

Delivery Area and Conditions: Currently, deliveries are made exclusively within the city area of Dubai (UAE). Deliveries to other Emirates or abroad are not possible at the present time.

Transport Insurance and Transfer of Risk: Each shipment is insured through our logistics partner under an insurance policy against loss and theft.

  • The insurance coverage exists up to a maximum goods value of USD 15 million per shipment. Shipments exceeding this value require a separate written agreement and confirmation by us in order to extend the insurance coverage accordingly.
  • The insurance ends, and the risk passes to the Buyer, as soon as the goods have been handed over at the delivery address and receipt has been confirmed by a physical signature (“Direct Signature Required”).
  • Identity verification: The logistics partner is contractually obliged to establish the identity of the recipient and the ultimate beneficial owner. A handover of the goods will therefore only take place upon presentation of a valid identification document (Emirates ID). The identity of the recipient must strictly match the data of the Buyer in the purchase contract.
  • A handover of the goods to an authorized representative (third party) is categorically permissible only if the Buyer has transmitted the fully completed and signed authorization letter (Authorization Letter) as well as a clearly legible copy of the authorized representative’s ID to the Seller in advance, and this has been approved by us to the logistics partner. The authorized representative must strictly present their own physical, original Emirates ID to the driver on site. Digital copies or photos of the ID on a smartphone will be rejected by the logistics partner without exception. The risk transfers to the Buyer as soon as the authorized representative has physically acknowledged receipt.
  • IMPORTANT: Delivery is made exclusively against personal handover. A drop-off authorization (e.g., “leave at the doorstep”) is not possible due to security guidelines. Any such authorization granted nevertheless will lead to the immediate expiration of the insurance coverage.

Upon the time of handover to the Buyer or the authorized third party, confirmed by a physical signature, the risk of accidental loss and accidental deterioration of the goods passes to the Buyer.

Duty of Inspection upon Receipt and Documentation: The customer is obliged to inspect the shipment immediately upon handover for external integrity and the integrity of the seals.   

  • Should the packaging appear damaged, opened, or manipulated (e.g., broken seals or taped-over security tape), the customer must strictly note these irregularities immediately on the delivery receipt of the courier. A signature without reservation shall be deemed confirmation that the goods were delivered and received in good condition and undamaged. Irregularities must be documented and captured photographically in the presence of the driver.
  • Duty to Cooperate: In the event of damage, the Customer is obliged to immediately provide us with all information and evidence necessary for claims processing (in particular, photos of the packaging and contents).
  • Reporting Deadline & Exclusion: Independent of the immediate notification at handover, concealed damages or shortages must be reported to us in writing via our online complaint form [Click here for the form] within 2 calendar days (48 hours) after delivery.

Important Notice: The customer acknowledges that an unconditional receipt or missing the 2-day reporting deadline means that transport damage can no longer be asserted against the insurance. This regularly leads to the rejection of liability and the loss of insurance coverage. After this period has expired, no more damages or shortages can be reported or asserted.  

Delivery and Refusal of Acceptance: The following conditions apply to the provision and acceptance of the goods:

  • The customer is obliged to be reachable at the address specified in the order at the agreed delivery time and to hold ready the documents (Emirates ID) required for identity verification (KYC).
  • Costs in Case of Unsuccessful Delivery: If the goods cannot be handed over for reasons for which the Customer or their authorized representative is responsible (e.g., absence, refusal of identity verification, refusal of acceptance, or failure to present the physical original ID of the authorized representative on site), the goods will be returned to the security warehouse of Loomis International. The costs arising from this for the return transport and the daily storage fees are to be borne in full by the Customer.
  • In the event of non-deliverability, our transport liability ends with the confirmed storage in the security depot of the logistics partner, and the risk of accidental loss and accidental deterioration of the goods passes to the Customer. A redelivery will only take place after full settlement of the additionally incurred fees. The costs for a redelivery are also to be borne in full by the Customer.

We reserve the right to pass on to the customer losses resulting from the delay in acceptance, as well as default interest specified in the contract with the logistics provider.
